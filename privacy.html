HomeHub privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

HomeHub is a family organiser for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your family's information stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. I don't run a server that receives it, I can't see it, and I don't use it for advertising, tracking or analytics.

What you put into HomeHub

You choose what to enter: names and photos of family members and helpers (including children), schedules, places and addresses, notes, dinner plans and shopping lists. HomeHub has no account or sign-in.

Where it is stored

  • On your device. Everything is stored on your iPhone or iPad.
  • In your iCloud, if you turn on Family Sharing. HomeHub uses Apple's CloudKit to keep family members' devices in step. The data lives in your own iCloud account, and in the iCloud of the people you invite. It is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. I have no access to it.

Optional features and what they use

  • Calendar. If you allow it, HomeHub can add your own drop-offs and pickups to a HomeHub calendar, and can show when you are busy. For that, it reads only the start and end times of events on the calendars you choose. It never reads event titles, places or attendees. Busy times for the next 14 days are shared with the family through iCloud only if you turn sharing on.
  • Weather. If you add a map pin to your Home place, its coordinates are sent to Apple Weather to get a forecast for clothing and bring-along suggestions. Nothing else is sent.
  • Maps. When you tap Open in Maps, the place's name or address is passed to Apple Maps.
  • Microphone and speech. If you use your voice with Ask HomeHub, speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recogniser, on your device where your device supports it. Questions are answered by Apple's on-device model where available.
  • Notifications. Reminders and briefings are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server.
  • Photos. If you pick a photo for a family member, it is stored in HomeHub and, with Family Sharing on, in your iCloud.

What I don't do

  • I don't collect, sell or share your information.
  • I don't use advertising, analytics or tracking, and HomeHub contains no third-party code that does.
  • I don't direct HomeHub at children. It is for parents and carers, who enter their children's details.

Deleting your information

In HomeHub, open Settings and choose Delete Family. This removes the family from the device, and from iCloud for everyone in the family. Deleting the app removes the copy stored on your device. If you were invited to someone else's family, you can leave it from Family Sharing in Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
