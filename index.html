HomeHub support

The family organiser for school runs, clubs and who is doing what.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: support@sedgehurst.co.uk. Please say which iPhone or iPad you use and which version of HomeHub (Settings, then About).

Common questions

How do I share HomeHub with my partner or a helper?

Open Settings, then Family Sharing, and invite them by iCloud. Everyone sees the same family schedule. Helpers only see their own responsibilities.

Two of my children go to places next door to each other. Can one adult do both?

Yes. Open Home, then Places, edit one place and choose the other under "Same stop, Right next to". Drop-offs and pickups at the two places within 30 minutes of each other become one trip.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Open Settings, then Reminders and briefings, and switch them on. If iOS notifications for HomeHub are turned off, use the link on that screen to open iOS Settings and allow them. Also check that "Whose device is this?" in HomeHub Settings is set to you.

My family's data isn't appearing on another device.

Both devices need to be signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on, and Family Sharing switched on in HomeHub. Changes can take a few moments to arrive.

How do I delete my data?

In HomeHub, open Settings and choose Delete Family. This removes the family from this device and from iCloud for everyone in it. Deleting the app removes the copy on your device. See the privacy policy for details.
